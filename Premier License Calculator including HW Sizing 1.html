How this is calculated

  • Premier uses node-based tier licensing and scales like Essentials/Advanced; ICMP-only nodes are free until additional monitoring is enabled. Cloud VMs ~3:1, containers ~10:1, thin APs 10:1 (confirm against the live license report).
  • WPM & SRM are included and unlimited in Premier today (Silicon 2026.2) and do not consume nodes; from Tungsten 2026.4, SRM is planned at ~5 nodes/array. IR Pro is bundled by tier; SaaS (NICO) aligns 1:1.
  • OSH poller count is the maximum across workload dimensions: elements ÷ protocol capacity (SNMPv2 100k / SNMPv3 72k / WMI 20k, at 16 cores), SAM components ÷ 40,000, flows ÷ 50,000 FPS, agents ÷ 1,000, and WMI's 2,000-node cap. Each instance supports up to 100 APEs and 1,000,000 elements.
  • Server hardware tiers (CPU/RAM) follow SolarWinds Platform 2026.2 system requirements; element-heavy pollers need 16 cores to reach the protocol caps, and more cores raise the cap further.
  • SaaS network devices are sized by the Network Collector (≤7,000 standard devices each; a standard device is SNMPv2 with ~15 interfaces). SaaS servers are monitored by the per-host Observability Agent (no central server; sends via OTLP 443) or agentlessly through a collector. Flow monitoring is ≤40,000 flows/sec per collector.
  • Recommended adds a headroom tier plus redundancy: HA database pair, an extra APE, an additional web server, and a standby / dedicated flow collector.
